## 0.1.0

- Initial pre-release. The out-of-process engine behind the `testsmith`
  CLI: VM Service transport, adb device control, flow and suite DSL,
  preflight, API-to-UI, Figma and visual validation, impact analysis,
  JSON and HTML reporting, and post-verdict AI analysis. Pure Dart; never
  depends on Flutter. The 0.x series may still change its API between
  minor versions.
